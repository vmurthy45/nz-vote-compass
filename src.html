How this works

  • Every party position comes from that party's own policy pages, or the full policy documents linked from them, read on . Nothing is taken from news coverage, voting records or guesswork.
  • If a party has not published a position on a statement, it is marked no stated position and that statement doesn't count towards its score.
  • Positions are coded supports or opposes when the policy says so directly. Leans is used when it is partial, conditional, or a goal to work towards. The quote shows exactly what the party said.
  • Your match is the average closeness between your answer and the party's position on the statements you both have a view on. Strongly agree against strongly disagree scores 0%; the same answer scores 100%.
  • Parties publish different amounts of detail. A match built on few statements is less reliable, so each row shows how many it rests on.
  • This is a guide to published policy only. Check the sources and the parties' full platforms before you vote.
