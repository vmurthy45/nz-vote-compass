How it works

  1. Every party position comes from that party's own policy pages, or the full policy documents linked from them, read on . Nothing comes from news coverage, voting records or guesswork.
  2. If a party hasn't published a position on a statement, it's marked no stated position and that statement doesn't count towards its score.
  3. Positions are coded supports or opposes when the policy says so directly, and leans when it's partial, conditional, or a goal to work towards.
  4. A party agrees with you when its position is within one step of your answer on the five-point scale, partly agrees at two steps, and disagrees at three or more.
  5. Your match is the average closeness across the statements you both have a view on. Opposite answers score 0%; identical answers score 100%.
  6. Parties publish different amounts of detail, so each result shows how many statements it rests on. Check the sources and full platforms before you vote.
